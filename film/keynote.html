Eval bug: CUDA mmvq fusion makes token generation 20-36% slower on Pascal (sm_61, MX230); GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_FUSION=1 is faster with identical output #32229
Openopened Nov 13, 2026

Problem description & steps to reproduce

On this Pascal GPU, token generation is 20–36% faster with GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_FUSION=1, and the output is unchanged.

modelfusion on (default)GGML_CUDA_DISABLE_FUSION=1change
Qwen2.5-0.5B Q4_044.0 t/s59.5 t/s+35%
Qwen2.5-1.5B Q4_019.8 t/s25.8 t/s+30%

The fused variant uses 80 registers/thread vs 66 unfused. I could not collect occupancy/stall counters to confirm the cause: nvprof --metrics fails on this driver even as root.

Cause not proven yet — the issue says so.

So we wrote our own.

How the model is stored: 18-byte blocks. They don't line up.
Re-packed: neat 16-byte rows. One big read each.
The old layout forces the chip to read in tiny 2-byte bites.
Small labels move to their own row. Same numbers, same math.
